How we work

Our name is our method.

Every project follows the same four steps.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We learn about your business, your customers and what you want to achieve.

  2. 02

    Extract

    We pull out the one thing that makes you different.

  3. 03

    Design

    We build the logo, campaign or publication around it.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    You get finished files, ready to print, post or publish.

About us

Who we are

A small, independent studio that cares about getting it right.

Extract & Design started in Colombo in 2015 as an independent team of designers. Our first big project was designing and publishing our own magazine, from the page layouts to the website.

Today we work with businesses, sporting clubs and publishers in Sri Lanka and Australia. You deal directly with the people doing the work.
